Book Information

The Book Nook features a curated collection of books. Book titles, authors, genres, publication details, and descriptions are included for educational and informational puproses.

Book cover images are the property of their respective publishers, authors, or rights holders. They are displayed to help visitors identify and discover books.

The website uses Playfair Display for headings and Open Sans for body text. Both fonts are available through Google Fonts.
